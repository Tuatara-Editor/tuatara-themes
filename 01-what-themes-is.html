What themes is

A package of popular colour themes for Tuatara, each a dark or light version of a palette you may know from other editors.

The package has fifteen themes. Each is chosen by its name, which M-x load-theme and (load-theme 'NAME) take.

Name (for load-theme)Palette
catppuccin-mochaCatppuccin Mocha, dark
catppuccin-latteCatppuccin Latte, light
draculaDracula, dark
nordNord, dark
one-darkOne Dark, dark
solarized-darkSolarized, dark
solarized-lightSolarized, light
tokyo-nightTokyo Night, dark
tokyo-night-lightTokyo Night Light, light
rose-pineRose Pine, dark
rose-pine-dawnRose Pine Dawn, light
everforest-darkEverforest, dark, medium contrast
everforest-lightEverforest, light, medium contrast
kanagawa-waveKanagawa Wave, dark
iroasetaIroaseta, dark

Dracula, Nord, One Dark and Iroaseta have no light version from their projects, so none is here. Catppuccin's Frappe and Macchiato, Rose Pine Moon, Kanagawa Dragon and Lotus and Solarized's other variants are left out.

It is a package, not part of the editor, and it needs no program.

1.1 Trying a theme

Once the package is loaded, M-x load-theme lists every theme, these included, and applies the one you choose. To have one at every start, put it after the package line in the init file:

(load-package "themes" :source "https://github.com/Tuatara-Editor/tuatara-themes")
(load-theme 'catppuccin-mocha)

1.2 A light and a dark theme

A pair of a light and a dark theme is what toggle-light-dark-theme switches between, and what the window follows when it follows the desktop's light or dark style:

(setf *dark-theme* 'tokyo-night
      *light-theme* 'tokyo-night-light)

Your own set-face lines in the init file stay on top of any theme. Chapter 4 lists the commands and settings.
