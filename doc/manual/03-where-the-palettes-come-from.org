* Where the palettes come from

The repository and commit each palette was read from, and the licences.

Each palette is read from the project's own repository at the commit named in
the table. Every project licenses it under the MIT license, and the notices are
in the =NOTICE= file at the top of the package's repository. Iroaseta is by the
author of this package, who made it for Helix. It is under this package's
licence here.
