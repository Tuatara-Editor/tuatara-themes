#+title: Choosing and pairing themes
#+language: en

* Choosing and pairing themes

The editor's commands and settings that choose and pair themes.

The package adds themes and nothing else: it has no command or setting of its
own. These belong to Tuatara and work with every theme, these included.
Tuatara's own manual describes them in the chapter on packages.

** Choosing a theme

=M-x load-theme= lists every theme, with a description beside each, and applies
the one you choose. Called from the init file as =(load-theme 'NAME)=, it
applies the theme at every start.

** Pairing a light and a dark theme

=M-x toggle-light-dark-theme= switches between a light and a dark theme. The
settings name the pair. If the window follows the desktop's light or dark
style, these are the two it switches between: =*light-theme*= and
=*dark-theme*=, which are Tuatara's own two themes until you set them.

#+begin_src lisp
(setf *dark-theme* 'tokyo-night
      *light-theme* 'tokyo-night-light)
#+end_src
